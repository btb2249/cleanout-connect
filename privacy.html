Privacy Policy — Cleanout Connect

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Cleanout Connect is made by Bethel Ventures. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. Cleanout Connect has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We do not collect, sell, share, or send your personal information anywhere — the app collects no personal data at all.

How the app works

  • You post cleanouts — estates, storage units, rental move-outs, evictions, garages and barns, offices — and browse cleanout postings right on your phone. Every posting you create — its description, location details, and phone number — stays on your device.
  • Crews and buyers contact you directly by phone. When you choose to call a number shown in the app, that call is made by your phone, not by the app, and the app does not record it.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your postings are stored in a private database on your phone only.
  • Photos you add to a posting are copied into the app's private storage on your phone only. They are never uploaded, emailed, or transmitted anywhere.
  • When you delete a posting, its photos are deleted with it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Phone contact

  • The app shows phone numbers only so you can call the person who posted a cleanout, only when you choose to call. It never accesses your contacts, call history, or messages on its own.
  • Any phone number you type into a posting stays on your device. The app does not send it anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • It does not take payments or ask for bank, card, or account information.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or messages.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.

Children: Cleanout Connect is made for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes: If a future version changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same address before the change takes effect.
